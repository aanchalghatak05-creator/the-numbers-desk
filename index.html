How we work

Find. Check. Explain.

Good data journalism makes its workings visible. We aim to link original sources, explain our decisions and correct mistakes transparently.

01 / Find

Start with a question that matters and locate the most authoritative available data.

02 / Check

Inspect coverage, definitions, missing values and limitations before drawing conclusions.

03 / Explain

Put the numbers in context with reporting, clear visuals and reproducible methods where possible.

About the desk

Independent, curious, accountable.

The Numbers Desk is an independent data-journalism project founded and edited by Aanchal Ghatak. It investigates public-interest questions around the world using data, interviews, public records and reproducible analysis.
